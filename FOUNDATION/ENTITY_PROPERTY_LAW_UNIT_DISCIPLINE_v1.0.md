# Ω-GROWER Foundation — How to Grow Entities Without Inventing Them

Status: FOUNDATIONAL METHODOLOGY v1.0

## Core rule

Ω-GROWER must grow candidate structures from relations without deciding in advance what physical thing they are supposed to become.

Correct direction:

`relations → boundary → candidate entity → persistence/invariant → property → interaction → law hypothesis → physical correspondence → measurement → unit`.

Incorrect direction:

`desired physical object → search for matching structure → promote resemblance as discovery`.

## Layer separation

The mental/informational research layer and the physical world are distinct. A candidate can exist as a model, relation pattern or computational structure without physical time or physical units.

Communication between the model and physical layer is an explicit correspondence hypothesis requiring evidence.

## Growth protocol

For every candidate:

1. Start from typed relations and observations.
2. Detect boundaries and stable substructures.
3. Record the neutral candidate before assigning a physical name.
4. Search for identity-preserving invariants.
5. Test perturbation and transformation stability.
6. Determine candidate properties from observed behavior.
7. Generate competing law hypotheses and nulls.
8. Test them in Ω-Lab.
9. Only after structural evidence survives, propose physical correspondence.
10. Define measurement procedure and unit only at the end.

## Promotion gate

A candidate must not be promoted to a physical entity because it matches a familiar concept by analogy.

Promotion requires:

`implemented → tested → falsification attempted → evidence → independent reuse → explicit physical correspondence`.

Human Gate retains reject/stop/redirect authority.

## Unit discipline

Never instruct the GROWER to discover a kilogram, metre or second as though these units were primitive objects.

The target is the underlying physical property or entity that can be measured. The unit is a later standard used to express the measurement.

## Time discipline

A candidate model may have:

- ordered states;
- transitions;
- recurrence;
- relative rates;
- memory/history;
- structural temporal measures.

Do not call these physical time without an explicit physical bridge.

## Required status language

Use the existing status vocabulary:

`CANDIDATE | TESTING | SUPPORTED | PARTIAL | NOT_PROVEN | FAIL | INVALID | ARCHIVED`.

Also preserve the distinction:

`FACT → CHECK → RESULT → DECISION → FIXATION`.

## Core principle

> The incubator grows structures first. It discovers what they are by testing persistence, invariants and interactions. Physical names, laws, measurements and units come only after the evidence establishes a justified correspondence.
